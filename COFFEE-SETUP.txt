BUY ME A COFFEE - 2 MINUTE SETUP
================================
The coffee button is already in the app (home page). It asks the visitor:
  1) how much they want to send
  2) the payment method

Card details are NEVER typed on your site. They are typed on the payment
provider's secure page. Never put your card number in any file.

YOUR STB RIB 10016131101497378895 IS ALREADY ADDED.
Optional: set DONATE_NAME to your name so it shows next to the RIB.

OPTION A - Send to your La Poste account (works right now, free)
  Set these 2 settings (Render dashboard > Environment, or your .env file):
    DONATE_NAME = your full name as on the account
    DONATE_CCP  = your CCP / RIB number (the ACCOUNT number, NOT the card number)
  The visitor sees them with a copy button and sends from their bank/La Poste/D17.

OPTION B - Card payment (Konnect, money goes to your Tunisian account)
  1) Create an account at https://konnect.network and add your La Poste / bank account
  2) Copy your API key and set:
       KONNECT_API_KEY = walletId:secret
       KONNECT_SANDBOX = false      (after you tested)
  The "Pay by card" option then appears by itself.

You can use A, B, or both.
