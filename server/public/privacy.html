Privacy Policy

Anonymous by design. Here is exactly what is processed.

What Orbyt does not collect

No accounts, names, emails, phone numbers, or profiles. Video and audio are peer-to-peer and are not recorded or stored by our server.

What is processed

  • Network address: used only to stop abuse and enforce bans. We keep a salted one-way hash, not your raw IP address. Bans are stored for up to the ban duration (default 72 hours).
  • Country: detected automatically from your connection using an offline database (no data is sent to a third party) and shown to the person you are matched with, together with your username or "Anonymous". It is approximate and can be wrong or hidden by a VPN. They never see your IP address through Orbyt.
  • Username: optional, chosen by you, shown only to the person you meet and kept in memory only while you chat.
  • Interests you type: held in memory only while you wait for a match.
  • Chat messages: relayed live and not stored. The last 20 messages of a conversation are kept in memory only so they can be attached if someone is reported.
  • Reports: reason, time, hashed identifiers and those last messages are stored in a log for moderation and legal compliance, and deleted after a retention period you choose (suggested: 90 days, unless needed for an investigation).
  • Relay servers: if a direct connection is impossible, your encrypted video passes through a TURN relay without being stored. Your IP is visible to the other person's browser during a direct WebRTC connection; use a VPN if that concerns you.

Cookies

Orbyt does not use tracking or advertising cookies.

Gifts and payments

If you choose to send a gift, card payments take place on the payment provider's own secure page. Orbyt never receives, sees or stores your card number, and keeps no record of who you are. Bank or postal transfers are handled entirely by your bank or the postal service.
