Safety Centre

How to have a good time and stay protected.

Orbyt is for adults (18+). If you are under 18, please leave. If you or someone else is in danger, contact your local emergency number.

Stay safe

  • Never share your full name, address, school, workplace, phone, social handles or passwords.
  • Don't click links or install anything a stranger sends you.
  • Nobody legitimate will ask for money, gift cards or crypto in a chat. Orbyt never asks for money inside a conversation; the only optional gift button is on the home page. A stranger who asks you to send money is a scammer: press Next and report them.
  • You can press Next any time, or Leave to stop. You don't owe anyone a conversation.
  • Remember: a stranger can record their screen. Don't do anything on camera you wouldn't want shared.

Report bad behaviour

Use the flag button in any chat and pick a reason. Reports for nudity and for people who seem under 18 are treated as the most serious. Two independent reports are enough for an automatic temporary ban.

Parents and guardians

Orbyt is not designed for children. Use your device's parental controls to block it, and talk to young people about the risks of talking to strangers online.

Live

Live streams are public and not pre-screened. Hosts and viewers must be 18+. Nudity, sexual content, anyone who looks under 18, harassment, hate, violence and scams are not allowed and lead to a ban. Use the flag button to report a live; hosts can remove a viewer or end their live at any time.
