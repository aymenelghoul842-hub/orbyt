1. Who may use Orbyt

Orbyt is for people aged 18 or older. By using the service you confirm you are an adult. If we believe someone is under 18 we may remove access immediately.

2. Rules

  • No nudity, sexual content, or sexual behaviour on camera or in text.
  • No harassment, hate, threats, doxxing, or sharing other people's private information.
  • No content involving minors in any sexual or exploitative context. This is reported to the authorities.
  • No spam, scams, advertising, recording or screen-capturing other users without consent.
  • No illegal content or activity.
  • Usernames must not impersonate staff, contain links, or be offensive. Your country is shown automatically to the person you meet.

3. Reports and bans

You can report the person you are chatting with at any time. Reports are reviewed automatically and by humans. Accounts and devices that break the rules may be banned temporarily or permanently, and a ban may apply to the network you are using.

4. Your video and chat

Video and audio go directly between you and the other person (peer-to-peer) and are not recorded by Orbyt. The other person is a stranger and could record you; never share personal information. If you submit a report, the most recent messages of that conversation are saved to investigate it.

5. No guarantees

Orbyt is provided "as is". We cannot control or verify who you will meet, and we are not responsible for what other users say or do, although we work to remove people who break these rules.

6. Law enforcement

We cooperate with valid legal requests and will report illegal content, including child sexual abuse material, to the relevant authorities.

7. Optional gifts

The “Buy me a coffee” button on the home page lets you send the operator an optional gift. Gifts are voluntary, unlock nothing, and are generally not refundable. Card payments are handled by a third-party payment provider on its own page; Orbyt never sees or stores your card details.
